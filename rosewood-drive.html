Rosewood Drive

A real band from 1992, restored rather than reinvented

Listen on Spotify Hear the story

Rosewood Drive is different from everything else on this site, and worth calling out plainly: it's a real band, from the early '90s, and I was part of it, not an AI composition. The songs on 1992 come from an old demo tape, recorded by an actual group of guys making an actual, if questionable, racket. What AI did here was restoration and completion, not invention: working through each track piece by piece to get as close as possible to what we actually sounded like, filling in what time and a cheap tape deck had worn away. I like to imagine this is what we would have sounded like if we'd had a little more talent, or tried a little harder at the time. The four songs were always meant to cover the wide range of our “sound” (for as much as the noise we made could be fairly called that) because coming out of the late '80s was a genuinely diverse moment for music, and we apparently couldn't commit to just one direction.

Being in a band in your early twenties feels like a rite of passage for a certain kind of young man, and I'm not sure I've entirely grown out of it, which is probably as honest an explanation as any for why this whole AI music experiment exists in the first place.
